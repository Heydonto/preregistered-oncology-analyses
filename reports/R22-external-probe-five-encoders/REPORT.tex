\section*{Summary}

R20 measured EAGLE against HTMCP on two encoders and reported Phikon-v2 1.0000 against
dinov2-large 0.8768, concluding the signature was encoder-independent in existence but not in
magnitude. R21 then showed that two-point claims in this series have twice been wrong. This was the
last two-point claim left, so it is now run on all five encoders.

\textbf{Verdict: EXTERNAL\_SIGNATURE\_PRESENT\_MAGNITUDE\_VARIES}, from R20's own thresholds,
hashed before R20's arms were read. Every encoder separates two institutions far above its
permutation null at $p = 0.0050$.

\section{The gate that halted, and what it found}

A first attempt at this run used a fresh seed. The reproduction gate compared its dinov2-large
value against R20's 0.8768, got \textbf{0.9240}, and halted.

That is not a coding difference. The seed sets the \texttt{StratifiedKFold} split, and on 76 slides
with roughly fifteen per fold a couple of reassignments move balanced accuracy by several points.
\textbf{R20's 0.8768 was one draw from a distribution with a standard deviation of 0.0247}, and it
sat near the bottom of it: the 25-split mean is 0.9176 and the observed minimum 0.8583.

So R20's substantive conclusion survives --- the signature is present under every encoder and
total only under some --- but its headline contrast was overstated. The honest gap between
Phikon-v2 and dinov2-large on these slides is \textbf{1.000 versus 0.918}, not 1.000 versus 0.877.
\textcolor{qred}{R20 is corrected.}

\textbf{What this run should have quoted from the start} is the distribution, not a point. Single-split
balanced accuracy on 76 samples is a noisy statistic and there was never a reason to treat it as
stable. The single-seed column is retained only to demonstrate exact reproduction.

\section{What the five encoders show}

\textbf{The signature is universal.} All five clear their nulls decisively, every null sits within
0.008 of chance, and every $p$ is at the floor of 0.0050 for 200 permutations.

\textbf{Phikon-v2 is genuinely saturated} --- 1.0000 on all 25 splits, standard deviation exactly
zero. That is not a lucky split; two institutions are perfectly linearly separable in its
mean-pooled feature space.

\textbf{The histology encoders cluster high and the natural-image encoder sits below them}: means
0.9601, 0.9937, 0.9961 and 1.0000 against 0.9176. The ordering matches R21's finding on methylation,
where the corpus split was much starker. Here all five are high, so the corpus effect is a
difference of degree rather than of kind, and it should not be leaned on --- with four histology
encoders spanning 0.960 to 1.000 and one natural-image encoder at 0.918, a single non-pathology
point is not enough to attribute the gap to corpus.

\textbf{Feature width is a confound on magnitude.} H-optimus-0 has 1536 dimensions and Virchow2
1280 against 1024 for the rest. A wider representation gives a linear probe more room on 76
samples. H-optimus-0 is near ceiling and Virchow2 is not, so width plainly does not order the
results --- but it is not controlled either.

\section{SET\_ALL, and the stain shortcut in the open}

Mixing the archive's 53 immunohistochemistry slides with the 76 H\&E ones raises dinov2-large from
0.8768 to 0.9444, exactly as R20 found. Adding a different stain makes the task easier, which is
the shortcut R12's audit predicted in writing. SET\_ALL is reported as an upper bound and is not a
measurement of site signal.

\section{Limitations}

\textbf{Two institutions, 76 H\&E slides.} This bounds existence, not effect size, and no number
here is a powered external benchmark.

\textbf{Slide-level mean features}, not the attention-MIL representation.

\textbf{Stain from filename suffixes} (\texttt{HE} / \texttt{CHR} / \texttt{P16}), the only stain
field available; source mislabelling propagates.

\textbf{One natural-image encoder.} The corpus ordering in \S3 rests on a single non-pathology
point and is stated as an observation, not a result.

\textbf{Ceiling effects make three of the five incomparable.} Phikon-v2, UNI and H-optimus-0 all
touch 1.0000. Differences among saturated measurements are not interpretable, which is a reason to
regard the whole external probe as a presence test rather than a ranking.

\textbf{Two of five are CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0} (UNI, Virchow2). Licences are recorded in the feature
files.
